

STEP 1 Notes: ADS1115 over I2C


Goal: Read a known voltage with the ADS1115 (16-bit ADC) through an Arduino
Uno over I2C, then verify it with a multimeter and a logic analyzer.


Parts:
 	Arduino Uno
	ADS1115 breakout (header pins soldered)
	Breadboard + jumper wires
	AstroAI AM33D multimeter
	HiLetgo 24 MHz 8-channel logic analyzer (PulseView)


Row		ADS1115pin

10		VDD
11		GND
12 		SCL
13		SDA
14		ADDR
15 		unused
16		A0	
17 		unused
18		unused	
19		unused		



Wiring:

	ADS1115   Row	Arduinho    Why 
			Uno

	VDD	  10a   5V	    Power
	GND	  11a   GND	    Common ref
	SCL	  12a   A5	    I2C clock
	SDA 	  13a   A4	    I2C data
	ADDR	  14a   GND	    Sets I2C address to 0x48
	A0 	  16a   3.3V        Known Test Voltage


Logic Analayzer:


	Logic analyzer	 Row	 Signal	    PulseView channel

	CH2		 13b	 SDA	    D1
	CH3		 12b	 SCL	    D2
	GND		 11b	 GND        __
